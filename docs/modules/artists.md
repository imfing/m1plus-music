# Artists

**Sources:** `patch/artists.c`.

**Genres and Composers are the same page** (`ACT_ARTISTS` with the row's stock Home tile as its argument: `artists_open(0)` Artists, `HOME_GENRE` 7, `HOME_COMPOSER` 8; `ar.cls` is the stock class listed: `0xf004`, `0xf006` `getAllGenre` (`select id,style from songtable group by style COLLATE NOCASE`), `0xf005` `getAllComposer`). The rows, the copy and the empty line ("Update Local Music first") are shared; the title is the menu row's text (`Genres` / `Composers`, the row's own key, translated in the 13 languages), the Artist / Album Artist button (`btn_artisttype`) is hidden (`visible=false` and `sensitive=false`: its slot takes no touch; a stray click is ignored, no setting written) and the `ar_sort` order is **not** applied: these two lists stay in stock's own order (the query's rows, then `toolsQueryDbTable`'s in-memory title sort: `getAllGenre` and `getAllComposer` both pass sort 1, comparator `0x5e0dac`; the "Unknown Genre" / "Unknown Composer" row, id -1, last). A row's name is `REC_TITLE` as the stock rows show (the genre row callback `0x4e9b80` stores the one string at `+0x1c` and at `+0x10`). A window that will not open, or an asset that is not ours, opens the tile's stock `localclass_page` through the Home dispatcher (`pod_home_open`), as the Songs lists do. A **genre's tap** is `songs_open_genre(name, unknown)` (`patch/songs.c`; the genre's songs on our Songs page, class `0xff09`, `docs/modules/songs.md` "A genre's songs"). A **composer's tap** is `albums_open_artist(list, row, CLASS_COMPOSERS)`: our Albums page scoped to the composer exactly as an artist's (below; Cover Flow is its navbar button), with `loadAlbumList(0xf005, row)` in place of `0xf004` and `albuminfo_page` opened with class `0xff02`; the stock evidence is in `docs/modules/addresses.md` ("a composer's albums"). The rest of this section describes Artists.

**The page is the Songs page's structure** (`docs/modules/songs.md`; the shared furniture is `patch/azlist.c`, below). `ui/m1plus_artists.bin` (`tools/pod_ui.py` `build_artists_page`): the navbar (the glass Back disc, the small title hidden at first, the switch, Now Playing), a stock `artists_listview` > `artists_scrollview` (310 wide, + `scroll_bar_m`) holding **one content view, `view_grid`**, that paints the rows, the section letters and the pinned header itself in its `EVT_AFTER_PAINT` (no widget per artist: the old page made a `list_item` + `hscroll_label` + `image` per row, 6000 widgets for 2000 artists, so it stopped at 2000; **`MAX_ARTISTS` is 20,000 now**: 5 bytes of the page's own per artist, 5000 artists open in 21.2 M payload instructions, the sort most of it: the test's names differ only inside digit runs, the costly case of the by-value compare; 5000 library-like names open in 20.0 M), and in it the large title `ar_title` (32 px white, x 12, 250 wide, insensitive); beside the list the rail `sg_rail` and the bubble `sg_bubble` (Songs' widgets and names, `azlist_rail()`), the hidden style labels `label_row_title` (21 px white: the stock `srlabel_leftwhite21_style`'s look), `label_hdr`, `label_rail`, `label_rail_on` (`azlist_labels()`), `img_nb_fade` and `label_status`. **The old names `pod_listview` / `pod_scrollview` are gone** (nothing but this page and its tests used them for the Artists asset).
- **Large title and collapse: the Songs pattern fits and is used.** The large title is the first thing in the content (`AR_TOP` = `SG_TITLE_H` 52 px, with no pills under it); the navbar's small title (`label_title`, 156 px before the switch) shows once the offset reaches `SG_COLLAPSE` (46, `pod_title_gone`), from the scroll_view's own `EVT_SCROLL`, with `img_nb_fade` shown while the list is scrolled (`ar_title_update`; without the scroll events the navbar keeps its title). Both titles are text set through `az_fit`: the mode's or the row's text cut with an ellipsis to its label (the Songs lists' rule). In the 13 languages only "Album Artists" is cut (ja, pl, ru, uk; "Album Artists" is wider than 156 px at the navbar's size); `test/pod/artists.py` measures all of them in the real font.
- **Rows**: 52 px (`AR_ROW`), the name 21 px white from x 24 in its 24 px box (`AR_TEXT_Y` 14: a row cut by the window's edge has its name cut too, by the `scroll_view`'s canvas clip, the Songs page's rule), cut with an ellipsis at x 252 (`AR_TEXT_END`: the Albums page's tile text, `grid_tile` / `grid_text` with no covers, a ring of `AR_TILES` 13), the chevron at x 262 (`AR_CHEV_X`, 10 x 16, a #8A8A8A bitmap the page makes at open: a two-segment stroke antialiased by 4 x 4 samples, because the stock `right_arrow` is 13 x 22 and `cc_decode` only makes squares), a `#222222` hairline from x 24 to 296, the pressed row filled `#333333`. There is no playing marker on this page (there never was one) and no cover. A tap (down and up within `SC_DRAG_PX` on the same row, as the Songs rows) calls `ar_open(display position)`: `albums_open_artist(list, ar_stock(i), scope)` for an artist or a composer (`coverflow_artist` with the same arguments if that returns -1, the Albums window not opening), `songs_open_genre(name, unknown)` for a genre; a touch on the title block, a header or the air under the last row opens nothing. The last 36 px (the rail's touch column, x 284) belong to the rail within its span.
- **Sections**: `ar_sections` gives every display position the rank of `cc_name_initial(name)` (the first byte of the text the sort compares: `so_name` of `patch/covercache.c`, white space and a leading `The ` dropped, so "The Beatles" files under B; a CJK character is its pinyin initial), `az_rank_of` (a letter A..Z either case; a digit, a symbol and every name in another script are "#", rank 0), and `az_sections` (`docs/modules/songs.md`, the non-decreasing labelling that puts the most rows under their own letter; the list keeps its order, a row out of place stays in the section around it, at most 27 sections, no memory = a plain list with no headers and no rail). **Artists** are sorted by `ar_sort` (below) and so are in letter order but for "Unknown Artist", other scripts (after Z) and digits and symbols (first); **Genres and Composers keep the stock order** (the in-memory title sort above; the "Unknown Genre" / "Unknown Composer" row last) and take the same labelling, so no second order is made. **The "Unknown ..." row (id -1) takes the rank of the row before it**: it is last in stock, would be a stray by its own letter and, as the last row of a run of one-row sections (a tie), could swallow the section of the row before it (a lone Z artist). The rail and the pinned header are `az_*`, unchanged: `sg_rail` at x 284, 36 wide, the compact A-Z plan, the grab and jump (to section i's header, clamped; the first section to the top, the large title with it), the bubble.
- **Order and the album Artist rule**: the order is the album Artist order's name rule, applied by `ar_sort`. The stock lists are sorted in memory by `toolsQueryDbTable` (sort 1, `algo_sort_if`, comparator `0x5e0dac`; not by the SQL `group by`): digit runs as numbers, `strcasecmp`, digits and symbols before letters, a CJK name through `utf8_to_pinyin` (`0x73a5f0`, `docs/modules/songs.md` "The Initial"). `cc_name_keys` of `patch/covercache.c` makes one key per name once per sort (the name without leading white space and a leading `The `, or a copy where each CJK character is its pinyin initial, taken by calling stock's `utf8_to_pinyin` on that one character; a block of 4 bytes per name plus the copies, one `calloc`), and `cc_key_cmp` compares keys: digit runs by value (leading zeros ignored), then per byte digits before symbols before letters before any non-ASCII byte, ASCII case folded; a prefix comes first; ties by stock position (`cc_heapsort`, no libc callback). Stock's second key for equal first initials (the full pinyin, `utf8_2_pinyin`) is not reproduced: CJK names compare by their initials string, other scripts by byte after the Latin names. `ar.order[]` (stock row at each display position, `calloc`s of 4 bytes per artist, the scratch arrays freed at once, `order` freed when the list is rebuilt and on close) is built over the copy, so no stock row moves. Every row but "Unknown Artist" (id -1) takes part as one set; the Unknown rows keep their stock positions (stock: last) and the sorted rows fill the other positions in order, so "The Beatles" reaches B wherever a fixed row sits. A failed allocation (order, row pointers or keys) leaves `order` NULL (stock order). `ar_stock` maps a display position to the stock row handed to `coverflow_artist`. The sort re-runs at every `ar_load` (open and the mode toggle). `getAlbumByArtist`, `getAlbumByAlbumArtist` (`0x4eaccc`, `0x4eb0e4`), `getAllComposer` (`0x4ebde8`), `getAllArtist`, `GetAllAlbumArtist`, `getAllAlbum` and `getAllGenre` all pass 1 as `toolsQueryDbTable`'s fourth argument (`addiu $a3, $zero, 1` before the call).
- **Cost against stock's sort**: stock's comparator, run for real under Unicorn on the same names (its libc calls mocked, counted and given rough glibc costs), takes per compare 744 instructions of its own code + ~2,100 of libc on the test's names (`Aa00012`), 1,484 + ~2,800 on library-like names, 1,445 + ~3,100 on CJK names; `cc_key_cmp` takes 148, 128 and 177. Each stock compare clears two 1 KB buffers, `snprintf`s both names, converts them to pinyin and calls `strlen` 18-41 times (in a loop condition); ours converts once per open (`cc_name_keys`, ~0.5 M for 1000 CJK names). A 5000-row sort (~61,000 compares, n log2 n assumed for `algo_sort_if`) is ~170-265 M for stock, 8-9 M for ours. Stock's sort always runs first (`loadLocalClassList`), so the page adds its whole open (20.0 M for 5000 library-like names, 21.2 M for the test's) to well over 10x that. The compare's common prefix is a plain byte loop (folding only bytes that differ) and digit runs are in `kc_runs` (one pass back over the common part, one forward): names that differ only inside a digit run cost 12-18 % more than the old ASCII-fold compare, library-like names 11 % less (22.5 M before).
- **The switch**: a navbar button in the toggle slot (`btn_artisttype`; the titles read "Artists" / "Album Artists", the glyph is the other mode's: `pod_nav_artist` / `pod_nav_albumartist`) does what the stock switch handler `0x535770` does: `artist_type` (`0xa05a14`) set, `write_int_config(v, "PLAYSET", "ARTISTTYPE")` (`0x4d6a2c`) and the byte `0xa06d20` (the stock Artists page's reload flag, read at `0x535fe8`) set to 1; then the list is queried again, rebuilt and scrolled to the top; the stock toast and style swap belong to the stock page and are skipped; Cover Flow and the album page read `artist_type` themselves. "Update Local Music first" for an empty library (the list and the rail hidden and insensitive).
- **Memory**: the copy of the stock rows (what the stock query returns, as before), `order` 4 bytes and `rank` 1 byte per artist, the 13 text tiles, the rail's 16 px disc and the 10 x 16 chevron (both freed with the window; `ar_on_destroy`). A tile ring that cannot be made closes the page (the tile's stock page opens for Genres and Composers, as an asset that is not ours).
Statics are prefixed `ar_`.

**Shared furniture, `patch/azlist.c` / `azlist.h`** (moved out of `patch/songs.c`, which keeps its behaviour and its tests): `az_sections` (the labelling), the geometry of a list with section headers (`az_hdr_y`, `az_row_y`, `az_row_from`, `az_at`, `az_span`, `az_max_scroll`), `az_paint_heads` (the letters and the pinned header), the rail (`az_rail_plan`, `az_rail_fit`, `az_rail_paint`, `az_rail_event`: the compact index of `docs/modules/songs.md`, its alignment from the tree's font, the grab and jump, the bubble, the disc), and `az_fit` (a title cut with an ellipsis). A page owns an `az_t` (the Songs page's `sg.al`, the Artists page's `ar.al`) and fills in the fields its comment marks `(page)`: its window, scroll_view, rail and bubble widgets, the three label styles, the top of the first header, the row height, the row count and the content height (what the offset is clamped to). `tools/pod_ui.py` shares the asset side (`azlist_labels`, `azlist_rail`); the Songs asset is byte for byte what it was.

**A tap opens our Albums page scoped to the artist** (`albums_open_artist`, `patch/albums.c`, `docs/modules/albums.md` "Scoped to an artist"): the page in the grid or list mode last chosen (the shared `albums-view` file), the artist's name as its title, that artist's albums and, with two or more, the All Songs tile last (our Songs page on every song, as the All albums card below), and the album tap opening the artist's album page as below. Its Cover Flow button (`btn_coverflow`) calls `coverflow_artist(list, index, scope)` for the same artist: the Cover Flow described next (`scope` `CLASS_ARTISTS`, or `CLASS_COMPOSERS` for a composer: `cf.scope`, what `loadAlbumList` takes, and, through `cc_open_album(row, scope, index)`, whether the album page opens as `0xff01` or `0xff02`): the same page, renderer, cache and touch layer scoped to
the artist (q2-pod's `ARTIST_ALBUMS`, `cf.scoped`). Everything below holds for a composer with that substitution (`0xf005`, `0xff02`, the composer in the title and on the card):

- **Albums** `loadAlbumList(0xf004, artist)`, copied into the page's deque with the artist's row.
- **Cards.** The stock list's last "All songs" row is kept as the **All albums** card with two albums
  or more (caption "All albums" over the artist, placeholder art, no cache file, tracks = every song
  of the artist), dropped with one.
- **Covers.** Each album takes the cache key of the library album of the same name (`strcasecmp`
  against `getAllAlbum`'s rows, grouped by name `COLLATE NOCASE`), so the page shows the main page's
  files and builds only covers the library lacks (a compilation's library row names whichever artist
  SQLite returned). With fewer than `IDX_MIN` 24 albums the match is that scan per album (the first library row of a
  name wins). From 24 albums up `load_artist` first builds a temporary case-insensitive index of the library's album
  names (open addressing, `calloc` of 8 bytes per library album rounded up to a power of two, hash = FNV-1a over the
  ASCII-folded bytes, equality `cc_nocase`, an equal name already in the table from an earlier row is kept: the first
  row wins) and looks every album up in it, then frees it; a refused allocation falls back to the scan. 200 albums over a
  1000-album library: about 10.2 M payload instructions (most of it the libc compares, `strcasecmp` counted as one
  instruction by the harness; roughly 180,000 string compares) before, 0.5 M with the index (`test/pod/artists.py`,
  which also pins the keys of both paths against a model, with case duplicates, a non-ASCII name and a missing album).
  Below 24 albums building the index (a pass over the whole library) costs more than the scans it saves, hence the threshold.
- **The album** (a centre album, not the All albums card) `loadAlbumList(0xf004, artist)` (the artist goes into the browsing record, the artist's
  albums and the stock All songs row into `*pdeq_showlist`), `albumcoverinfo_init`, `batchadd_albumcover_task(0, 0)`, then our album page, else
  `navigator_to_with_context("albuminfo_page", 0xff01 | row << 16)`, the context the stock artist page's row
  handler builds (`0x523d94`); the page runs `loadAlbumdetailList(0xff01, row)` itself and leaves class
  `0xff11`. On every open, as in the main Cover Flow. The **All albums card** opens our Songs page on every song of the artist (`songs_open_artist`, the Songs button's call; the stock page on the All songs row if it will not open).
- **Songs button.** `btn_songs` (the left cap of the Songs | Now Playing capsule at 214) opens our Songs page on the artist's songs (`songs_open_artist`: `loadAlbumList(0xf004)` + `loadAlbumdetailList(0xff01, the All songs row)`, class `0xff11`; docs/modules/coverflow.md, songs.md).
- **Ends.** The shelf does not wrap (ring slots past either end stay empty, drag clamped, fling and
  side taps stop at an end); the main page still wraps.
- Title is the artist; the scope never reads or writes `/mnt/mmc/.m1plus/coverflow-position`; "No albums" when
  empty; Back goes covers -> Albums page (when opened from its button) -> Artists -> menu; Cover Flow is never stored as the mode. The stock list always ends with "Unknown
  Artist" (id -1), which opens to "No albums" without untagged songs. A refused second open while Cover
  Flow is open.

**Row recycling: resolved by the painted content view** (it was "investigated, not done" in the V2.1nL performance review, item 6: one `list_item` + `hscroll_label` + `image` per artist, 6000 widgets and 11.5 M payload instructions for 2000 artists, the cap). The page makes no widget per row any more (the Songs page's structure above), so there is nothing to recycle: a paint walks only the rows in view (`az_span`), the text ring holds 13 tiles, a tap is a position computed from the touch (`az_at`) instead of a click context per row (blockers 1 and 4 of that note, the per-row click context and the mocked `list_item`, are gone with the rows), and no press is held by a row widget (blocker 2). 5000 artists open in 21.2 M payload instructions (`test/pod/artists.py`); the cap is `MAX_ARTISTS` 20,000.

Borrowed from q2-pod: the scope, the bounded shelf, the All albums rule; not borrowed: its hook on the
stock artist page and its own SQL album list (here the stock page's own list and the stock All songs
row, so no hook). Risks: the flat fallback is not bounded; renamed albums mid-rescan get their own key; the page's look (the chevron's stroke, the rows' x, the large title on Genres and
Composers) is not seen on the device yet. Future: a Sort card (the stock rows carry an index letter at `+0x54`, `docs/modules/songs.md` has its encoding; the page's sections come from the name instead, so a CJK name is "#").
