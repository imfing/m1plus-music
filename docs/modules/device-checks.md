# Device checks

Record the build tag, device/card, result (pass/fail/untested) and capture for each applicable
check. This is a checklist, not a record of completed testing. Historical observations stay
in [history](../history.md); feature modules retain their detailed edge cases.

## Library and playback

- **ALB-01 — Albums:** switch grid/list in place, retain the first visible album and mode after

  taps, first cache build, cancel and rebuild. The navbar stays usable. [Details](coverflow.md)
- **CF-VOL — Cover Flow volume buttons:** Settings > Library > Volume Buttons on. In Cover Flow
  (portrait, then landscape; the main shelf and an artist's) volume down moves one album right and
  volume up one left, quick presses add up, the ends stop (no wrap), no volume dialog appears and
  the volume does not change. Note whether holding a button keeps stepping (kernel key repeat).
  Pod Cover Flow still changes the volume. Switch off: the volume dialog and volume change are
  back. Screen off: a press wakes/behaves as stock. Also with `ROTATEMODE=1` (up/down swapped).
  [Details](coverflow.md)
- **COMP-01 — Composer page:** Composers > a composer with two or more albums: the centred name and the
  Play / Shuffle pills (no circle, no photo), each album's year under it, All Songs with no line. Play
  plays every song of the composer from the first, Shuffle from a random one (stock class 0xff14, not
  run on a device before): Now Playing and the Queue show the composer's songs. All Songs (the tile and,
  in Cover Flow, the Songs button and the All albums card) opens our Songs page titled with the
  composer, each row's grey line its artist; a row plays from there, Select > Add to Playlist works.
  [Details](albums.md)
- **SONG-01 — Songs:** title collapse, pinned letters, all rail sections/bubble, search, each
  sort, select-all, add/delete and return position. Check a large library; covers load after

- **NAV-01 — Album/navbar:** one seamless capsule, contained pressed highlights and no leftover

- **PLAY-01 — Now Playing/lock screen:** sharp rounded covers, tint changes, neutral missing
  art, remaining time, transport/favorite/More controls, translucent card and swipe behaviour.
  Check idle/no-queue and charging states. [Now Playing](nowplaying.md), [lock screen](lockscreen.md)
- **NEXT-01 — Continue Playing:** Settings > Playback > Continue Playing > Next Albums (the row then shows Albums). Albums (Artist order): Play an

## Home content

- **HOME-01 — Mini player:** absent with no queue and on the normal menu; appears within a
  tick for playback. Check long titles, transport/track updates, Now Playing return, dialogs,
  pull-down and the final row scrolling clear above it. [Details](minibar.md)

  rows without layout jumps, lazy cover loads and no stutter. Check missing/deleted albums,
  taps versus drags, coasting catch, setting persistence and reboot. [Details](recent.md)
- **HOME-04 — Daily Mix:** responsive creation/regeneration, readable card over varied palettes,
  expected songs/artist limits, play/shuffle/track taps and correct detail page. Check settings,
  reboot, card swap/no card, missing covers and Add-to-playlist behaviour. [Details](dailymix.md)
- **HOME-05 — Cache pill and sync icon:** with covers missing (Developer > Clear Cover Cache, then Home) the pill "Checking library…" (empty bar, no count; "Rebuilding covers…" after Rebuild Covers) appears at once, and about 5 s after Home is in front it becomes "Preparing covers n/m"
  with the bar growing and the list below it intact; "Measuring tracks n/m" follows. The x stops the pass at once without a stall, the pill hides, and the pass does not return
  on leaving and re-entering Home until a reboot or Settings > Library > Rebuild Covers (which then runs). While any build runs, on Home, Albums, Songs, Cover Flow and Settings, the static sync glyph
  shows between Bluetooth and Wi-Fi in the status bar and in the pull-down's bar, clear of the clock (also with Battery Percentage on); it goes out when the build ends or is stopped. Playback and scrolling stay smooth during
  a build (the worker runs at nice 10). With the cache complete, leaving and re-entering Home shows no pill and does no scan; after playing a song no pill shows (same library); adding an album shows it about 5 s later and builds; the x while it waits cancels it, and after a reboot it returns. Leaving Home mid-build (or during the wait) and coming back restarts it after about 1 s, not 5. Check the pill's text and bar in several languages. [Details](homecover.md), [sync icon](lineout.md)

## Navidrome

Module: [Navidrome](subsonic.md). Use a Navidrome server on the LAN (http) and one with a valid certificate (https), a library of a few hundred albums
including FLAC, a DSD track and a playlist; note the Wi-Fi state for each check.

- **ND-01 — Sign In:** with no server the Subsonic root shows the empty state with an Add Server pill; it opens the Sign In page (three rows: Server URL, Username, Password; our keyboard, the action key reads Next, then Sign In on the last row). Enter the URL over http, then over https; the password is masked (dots). Sign In pings with the typed login and shows a spinner and "Connecting…". A wrong password shows the grey line "Incorrect username or password" under the card, an unreachable host "Can't connect to the server", a bad certificate its own line; after a failure nothing is saved (`subsonic.conf` is absent or unchanged). A good login saves `subsonic.conf`, starts a sync and returns to the root, whose status line reads Syncing… then Synced.
- **ND-02 — Server page:** after a sign-in `/mnt/mmc/.m1plus/subsonic.conf` holds the address, the user and 48 hex digits, never the password (a card that still has an older file with the password in it stays signed in, and the file is written again without it at the first open); each field row opens the Sign In page on that row; the password is masked; Sync Now and Sign Out ask first and Cancel changes nothing. Signed out: Add Server (and "..." > Server Settings) opens Sign In directly, a good sign-in returns to the root showing Syncing… and fills in; Sign Out returns to the root. Tapping Sync Now on the Server page shows the spinner and "Syncing…" (row grey) at once, then "Synced just now"; the root's status line says Syncing… as soon as you confirm from its "..." card.
- **ND-03 — Sync:** open the root with the cache older than 15 minutes and Wi-Fi up: the status line shows the spinner and Syncing... then the check circle and
  "Synced just now", with "N songs" as the footer under the rows; "..." > Sync Now repeats it (grey while it runs). Wi-Fi off: the plug glyph, no sync starts, Sync Now toasts "Connect to Wi-Fi first". Scrolling the root (and the Server page) collapses the large title into the navbar.
- **ND-04 — Browse:** Albums (grid and list), Artists (A-Z, an artist's albums), Playlists (grid; a playlist page with a mosaic when it has no cover),
  Songs, Favorites and Downloaded all list the server's rows; scrolling the Songs list to its end loads more. The bars show only what the source can do:
  Albums has "..." and Now Playing (no Cover Flow, no Search row in its card); Songs, Favorites and Downloaded have Back, the title to x 260 and Now Playing
  (no Search, no Select, Now Playing a plain disc). The Playlists tiles show each playlist's count at once, also before a playlist is opened and with Wi-Fi off.
- **ND-05 — Album page:** cover loads for visible rows only; the footer shows tracks and minutes; the track list matches the server.
- **ND-06 — FLAC stream:** play a FLAC album track: Now Playing and the stock player show the sample rate and bit depth of the file; seek forward and back
  and the time follows; the next track plays from the queue (up to 64 tracks). Tap track 5 of a 12-track album: Previous from track 1 and Repeat reach tracks 1-4, which
  follow track 12 in the queue. Shuffle on that album queues all 12 once; on a list over 64 songs it queues 64 spread over the whole list.
- **ND-07 — Play Next / Play After with a stream:** from a streamed track's "..." sheet, Play Next then Play After put that track in the queue at the right
  place; Now Playing shows it in order.
- **ND-08a — Now Playing on a stream:** skip from one streamed track to the next: the old cover stays until the new one shows (stock's placeholder never flashes in between), including a track
  whose cover was not cached; an album's next track is usually already cached. On the info page File Size is the server's size and Location is the server address only (no token, user or path); a downloaded track shows its file's own lines.
- **ND-08b — Playback pages on a server track:** play a Subsonic album (streamed) whose artist and album also exist on the card, with different covers: Queue page, the Home mini bar and the lock screen's mini bar show the server's cover (as Now
  Playing), not the card album's; the timed cover page (ten idle seconds on Now Playing, Idle Display = Album Cover) shows the server's cover at 320 px (soft, the file is 168 px), stock's page meanwhile for an uncached one, and no stale cover of the
  previous local track. Now Playing's star: tap it on a streamed and on a downloaded server track: the star fills, "Added to Favorites" shows, the server has the favourite (web UI) and Settings > Favorites / the stock Favorites list has no new
  entry; tap again to undo; Wi-Fi off: the change is refused and the star goes back. A local track's star still adds to stock Favorites. More > More > Cover & Lyrics is dimmed on a server track (Wi-Fi on) and live on a local one. Not run on a device.
- **ND-08 — Playing marker:** the album page and the Songs list mark the server track that is playing.
- **ND-09 — Download and resume:** Download a track from the sheet: the row's download ring fills clockwise (no percentage text), a stop square over it while it runs; turn Wi-Fi off mid-transfer: the ring stops where it was; Wi-Fi on again: the download resumes from the `.part` file (not from zero) and the badge shows Downloaded. The file is in `/mnt/mmc/Subsonic`.
- **ND-10 — Cancel and Remove:** Cancel Download on a waiting or running row removes the `.part` file; Remove Download on a finished row deletes the file
  and its row leaves Downloaded.
- **ND-11 — DSD confirm:** tap an undownloaded DSD track (row, Play, Shuffle): the dialog reads "Download to Play?" with "DSD tracks play from the card.
  <title>, <size> MB." (a long title is cut with three dots, the text wraps cleanly to its lines), buttons Cancel and Download; Download starts the file.
- **ND-12 — Downloaded DSD:** the downloaded DSD track plays from the card (its tag shows) and the file appears in the SD library (Albums or Songs of the
  card) without a reboot.
- **ND-13 — Offline:** with Wi-Fi off: Downloaded plays; other streamable rows are dimmed and their tap toasts "Connect to Wi-Fi first"; a list never
  fetched shows an empty status; the Playlists tiles still show their sync counts, and a playlist that was never read shows the count but no rows when opened.
- **ND-14 — Favorite:** Favorite on a track's sheet toggles on the server (check the web UI or another client); Undo Favorite reverts it; with the Wi-Fi
  off the change is refused and the row reverts.
- **ND-15 — Get Info:** Get Info for a remote track and for a downloaded track: both show the title, album, artist and length.
- **ND-16 — Sign Out:** Sign Out clears the Subsonic lists and covers, the root shows the empty state again (Add Server, and a Downloaded pill), and the downloads and `dl.idx` remain: Downloaded
  still lists and plays them. Sign Out with a download waiting or running stops it (no download resumes later), and a server stream that is playing stops;
  a downloaded track that is playing keeps playing.
- **ND-17 — Old files:** with `navidrome.index`, `navidrome.index.tmp`, `navidrome.dsd.tmp`, `navidrome-art/` and `navidrome-view` on the card, open
  the Subsonic root once: all are gone; reopen it: nothing else is removed; `subsonic.conf` and `/mnt/mmc/Subsonic` are untouched.
- **ND-18 — Standby during playback:** a keep-awake tick runs while a stream plays (see [subsonic](subsonic.md#playback-and-the-queue)). Start a long
  Navidrome album (over 30 minutes) and let the screen sleep: record whether the device sleeps, whether playback continues and what auto power-off does.
  Compare with a local album.
- **ND-20 — Demo server regressions:** against `https://demo.navidrome.org` (user and password `demo`): Favorites rows show their covers; scrolling the Albums grid keeps the loaded covers (no flash to the placeholder,
  the fling is not stopped); tapping an album lists its tracks, with the header cover and the tint, also right after fast scrolling; Songs fills page by page to the end of its first screens (no "No songs"). With Wi-Fi off at the tap,
  turn it on: the tracks appear within about 10 seconds without leaving the page.
- **ND-21 — Background fill on a big library:** against a library of several thousand songs (5,000 to 50,000 if one is at hand; note the song and album counts): sign in, open the Subsonic root and leave it open. The status line turns from Syncing… to "Updating library… N/M" (songs), then "Loading covers… N/M", then "Synced just now". Record the time of each stage and of the whole run, the card space used by `/mnt/mmc/.m1plus/subsonic` (`songs.idx` plus `al/` take about 1.6 KB a song) and the battery drop. While it runs, use the device: open an album, an artist, Search and play a track (each must open at once or after at most a few seconds, never waiting for the fill; the album opens with no spinner once the derive has run), start a download (the fill pauses), tap Sync Now (the sync runs first, the fill continues), scroll the Albums grid (covers already there; nothing stutters). Afterwards: the Songs list is complete with its A-Z (up to 10,000 rows), an album you never opened opens with its tracks and no spinner (airplane-mode check: turn Wi-Fi off first), covers of every album show. Interrupt it three ways and reopen the root each time: Wi-Fi off mid-run (it ends quietly, the line returns to the age), a power-off mid-run, Sleep for a minute; the line resumes with the counts continuing (the covers' total restarts from what is left), and `bg.txt`/`songs.part` show no refetching from zero (watch the server log if possible: no repeated offsets). Sync Now twice with an unchanged library: no `search3` or `getCoverArt` in the server log. Sign Out mid-run: the line stops, the `subsonic` folder is emptied of cache and `bg.txt`/`songs.part` are gone. Not device-tested. Also: on a server that refuses `getPlaylists` (a user without playlist rights) the sync still ends "Synced just now" and the fill runs; Playlists shows its old list or none.
- **ND-19 — Songs cap:** on a library over 10,000 tracks, the Songs list ends at 10,000 rows, with no A-Z rail; a library under the cap shows the rail.
- **ND-22 — Big playlist and many favourites:** on a Navidrome server with a playlist of about 3,000 tracks and about 1,500 starred songs (more than the old 390-track / 512 KiB limit): sync, then open that playlist from Playlists: the rows arrive (a spinner for a few seconds is fine), the title shows the track count and the last row is the playlist's last track; tap a row in the middle and Play: the queue starts on that track (a window of 64 tracks in the playlist's order from the tapped one, the known limit). Open Favorites: every starred song is listed. Note the time to open and whether the device stays responsive while the reply lands (a 3,000-row list is about 2.5 MB parsed). Afterwards a Home/Albums visit and a second playlist open work (the response buffer is freed and grown again). A playlist whose reply passes 4 MiB (about 3,000 long-tagged tracks) fails with the list unchanged, and one over 3,000 rows that fits keeps its first 3,000. Not device-tested.
- **ND-23 — Track covers after the fill:** on a Navidrome library whose songs have embedded art (Navidrome gives those songs their own `mf-` cover ids), sign in, let the Background fill finish ("Synced just now") and note the number of files in `/mnt/mmc/.m1plus/subsonic/covers`. Then open songs from an album, the Songs list (scroll it), Search (songs), a playlist (Playlists grid and the playlist's header), Favorites, play a track and visit Now Playing, the Queue, the mini bar, the timed cover page, Vinyl and the lock screen: every cover is there at once with no grey spinner, and the file count has not grown (a library with a song whose album has no cover may add that song's own file). With Wi-Fi off the same pages still show their covers. After a second Sync Now on an unchanged library the covers still show at once. Not device-tested.
- **ND-24 — Artist pictures:** on a Navidrome server where some artists have an image (an `artist.jpg` in the artist folder, or an external image from Last.fm / Deezer) and some do not, sign in, let the Background fill finish and watch the root's status line: after "Loading covers… N/M" it shows "Loading artist images… N/M" (the numbers count the pictures that were missing; no line at all when none was), then "Synced just now". Record the time of that stage and the files added under `/mnt/mmc/.m1plus/subsonic/covers`. Open Artists: the rows with a picture show it in the 40 px circle (compare the look with a library artist's photo: same circle, same edge), the rest the grey placeholder with the microphone; scroll fast (the circles must not stall the list; a decode is a few ms a tick). Open an artist with a picture: the 88 px circle header as a library artist with a photo, a tall portrait picture cut with the face near the top, a wide one centred; an artist without one: the name and pills higher with no circle and no grey disc. **The placeholder question:** open an artist the server has no image for (check on the server: Navidrome serves its own silhouette for it with HTTP 200 and `Cache-Control: no-store`, a WebP): the row must show the app's grey placeholder, not a silhouette or a stretched grey picture, and `covers/` must hold an empty file for it; if another server answers a generic picture as JPEG or PNG, note which server and what shows. Before the fill ends (Sync Now, then Artists at once): rows and the page show the placeholder with a small grey spinner on the row and the picture appears without reopening; the page's circle appears in place while it is open. With Wi-Fi off the cached pictures still show and no spinner turns for the rest. Not device-tested.
- **ND-25 — Standby during a download:** with Subsonic signed in and the screen idle, queue a large download (a long FLAC or several albums), do not play anything and leave the device alone with the screen timeout short. The download must carry on with the screen off, without the device going into standby (Wi-Fi stays up; the file finishes and the ring ends in the Downloaded mark), and after the last file ends the device goes to standby by its normal timeout. Also with the queue full (33 adds): the 33rd says "Download queue is full". Not device-tested.
- **ND-26 — Download Album:** with Subsonic signed in and Wi-Fi up, open a server album of several tracks (include one DSD track if the library has one). The navbar shows Back, the "..." and Now Playing as one capsule where the library's album page has Select. Tap "...": a small card with one row, Download Album, and its download glyph; tapping the dimmed page outside it closes the card. Tap Download Album: the card closes, one toast "Downloading N songs" (N = the tracks, DSD included; "Downloading 1 song" for a one-track album) and the rows show their waiting / progress marks in list order; the files arrive one at a time and the screen can go off meanwhile (as ND-25). Open the album again after some finished and run it once more: only the missing tracks are queued (the toast counts them); when all are on the card or queued: "Already downloaded". With Wi-Fi off: "Connect to Wi-Fi first" and nothing queued. With a long queue (an album, or several albums downloaded in a row, past 32 files): "Download queue is full" and the tracks that fit are queued. Open a library (card) album: its navbar is unchanged, Select where it was and no "...". Not device-tested.
