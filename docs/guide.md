# M1+ Music user guide

This guide will help you find your music, play it and choose what plays next. If you haven't
installed M1+ Music yet, start with [Install](install.md).

## Start listening

1. Turn off the player, copy your music to the microSD card, then insert it in the player.
2. On Home, open **Extras → Update Library** and tap **Update Now** to scan the card.
3. Go back to Home and open **Albums**, **Artists** or **Songs**.
4. Tap a song to play it.

After you add or move music on the card, run **Update Now** again so the player can find it. To add
only new music in a few folders, use **Choose Folders**, tick the folders and tap **Scan**. Update
Library shows how many songs you have and when the library was last updated. The page shows when the library was last updated; **Auto Update** does it for you after a card is inserted or a USB transfer ends.

## Find your way around

Home is where you choose what to listen to. It opens with Cover Flow, Albums, Artists, Songs,
Playlists and Extras. Scroll down for Daily Mix and Recently Played. Anything you pin appears above
Cover Flow.

- **Tap** an item to open it. Drag a list up or down to scroll.
- Tap **Back** at the top left to return to the previous page.
- Tap **…** for more choices on a page or a song row.
- Open **Extras**, then tap the Settings button at the top to change how the player works.

While music is queued, a mini player appears at the bottom of Home. Tap its cover to open
Now Playing, or use its buttons to pause and skip without leaving Home.

## Browse and play

![Cover Flow, Albums and Playlists](screenshots/library.jpg)

*From left: Cover Flow, Albums and Playlists.*

### Play an album

1. On Home, tap **Albums**, then tap an album.
2. Tap a song to start there, or tap **Play all** to start at the beginning.
3. To shuffle the album, tap the shuffle button beside **Play all**.

To browse by artist, open **Artists** instead and choose an artist, then an album. In Cover Flow,
drag through the covers and tap the centre cover to open its album. Songs tagged with several
artists or genres can be listed under each name with **Settings → Library → Split Artists** and
**Split Genres** ([library guide](library-guide.md#split-artists)).

### Pin to Home

Pin up to six albums, playlists, songs or artists to the top of Home, above Cover Flow. Tap **…**
(or hold the item) and choose **Pin Album**, **Pin Playlist**, **Pin Song** or **Pin Artist**;
**Unpin** takes it away. Tap a tile to open or play it, or hold it for **Play**, **Shuffle**, **Unpin**
(and **Add to Playlist**, **Play Next** and **Play After** on an album or a playlist). To reorder, hold a tile until it lifts and drag it to another place. A pinned artist
stays in the list it came from (**Artists** or **Album Artists**); with Split Artists off, a pinned
guest artist says "Can't play" until the setting is on again. If the card cannot be read or
written, pinning says "Can't update pins" and the list is left alone (delete `.m1plus/home-pins`
to start again). With six pinned, unpin one before pinning another. A pin stays until you
unpin it: if its item is no longer in the library, tapping it says "Not found".

### Find a song

Open **Songs** and use the A–Z rail to jump through the list. To search, tap the magnifying glass
in the Songs page's top bar and type a title, artist or album. Touch and hold a result for its menu: **Play Next**, **Add to Playlist** and more on a song, the album and playlist menus, and **Play**, **Shuffle** and **Pin Artist** on an artist.

### Change the view or order

On Albums, Artists, Songs or Playlists, tap **…** for the options available on that page. For
example, in Albums you can switch between grid and list or sort by title, artist, date added or year.

### Select several items

On Songs, Folders, Playlists, Podcasts or an album, choose **Select** (on an album, in the **…** menu), then tap items to mark them.
**Select All** (top right) marks everything. The icons in the pill at the bottom act on the marked items:
add to a playlist, or delete (the trash can; in a playlist it removes the songs from that playlist only). They stay dim until something is marked. Tap **×** to leave.

In a playlist, open **…** and choose **Edit** instead: the Shuffle and Play buttons make way, the bar shows delete and **+**, and tapping the playlist's name (the pencil beside it) renames it, and tapping the cover (the round pencil on its corner) opens the **Playlist Cover** page: choose Albums (the mosaic of the playlist's albums) or one of five generated looks (Solid, Mesh, Sunburst, Stripes, Band) in colours taken from the albums; a tap takes effect at once, and **Name on Cover** writes the playlist's name (up to two lines) in white at the top left of the cover, on the playlist's page, the Playlists grid and a pinned playlist on Home. Press the three-bar handle at the right of a song to lift it, then drag it to a new place (near the top or the bottom edge the list scrolls by itself); the order is saved when you let go. A new playlist opens straight away with an **Add Songs** row; tap it to fill the playlist. Tap **+** to add songs: the library opens with a plus beside every song (a grey check for songs already in the playlist), and one tap adds a song at the end; the check mark at the top left comes back to the playlist. Tap the check mark at the top left when you are done.

## Control playback

![Now Playing, lyrics and an album](screenshots/playback.jpg)

*From left: Now Playing, lyrics and an album's songs.*

### Open Now Playing

Tap the mini player's cover on Home, or tap the Now Playing button in a page's top bar. On Now Playing:

- Use the centre button to pause or resume, and the buttons beside it to skip tracks. The cover shrinks while paused and springs back when the music resumes; turn this off in Settings → Now Playing → Shrink on Pause.
- Swipe across the cover to see lyrics, then swipe again for song details. While a podcast episode or an audiobook plays, the first swipe shows the episode's notes instead of lyrics (scroll them with a finger); the file details are on the next page.
- Tap **…** for more options, including display modes and the Sleep Timer (a moon icon appears in the status bar while a timer is running, when the bar has room for it).

### Control Center

Pull down from the top edge, then swipe left for a second page with the playing track, its controls and a few shortcuts. Play Mode cycles List play, Repeat one, Shuffle and Repeat all; Up Next, Go to Artist and Go to Album open those pages; the Equalizer and Sleep cards show their state and open their pages. The pull-down opens on the page you last left it on; after a restart it starts on the first page.

While a podcast episode or an audiobook plays, the page shows its own controls instead: back 15 seconds, play / pause and forward 30 seconds on the card; a Speed card (tap to step through the speeds; it stays on the pull-down) in place of the Equalizer; and four shortcuts for Podcasts (or Audiobooks), Up Next, the episode's notes (or the book's page) and the next episode or part. The lit buttons take the accent colour.

While a radio station is up, the page shows the station's logo, name and line with one Stop / Play button and a LIVE divider. The four buttons open My Stations, Recently Played, Station Info and Search; the Station card adds or removes the station in My Stations; Sleep stays, and there is no Equalizer card.

### Change the volume

Tap the status bar at the top of any page to open **Volume**. Use the **−** and **+** buttons, drag
the bar, or press the volume keys. A small mark on the bar shows the **Max Volume** limit. Under
the bar, shortcuts open **Max Volume**, **Equalizer** and **Volume & Sound**. If you open it by
accident too often, turn off **Tap for Volume** in Settings > Buttons & Power.

### Choose what plays next

1. On an album or Songs list, tap a song's **…**, or touch and hold its row. To queue a whole album, tap **…** on the album, or touch and hold it on **Albums**, **Recently Played**, the **Recently Added** album grid or your Home pins. The same works on a playlist on **Playlists** or among your Home pins.
2. Choose **Play Next** to hear it after the current song, or **Play After** to add it to the end.

To see the order, tap the queue button at the bottom right of Now Playing. In **Up Next**, tap a
song's **…** to move or remove it. Removing a song from the queue doesn't delete its file.

## Connect to Wi-Fi or Bluetooth

1. Open **Extras → Settings**.
2. Tap **Wi-Fi** or **Bluetooth** and turn it on.
3. Tap a network to connect, or tap a nearby Bluetooth device to pair it.

Wi-Fi is needed for features such as Radio and online cover lookup. For more connection options,
see [Settings](settings-guide.md#wi-fi).

## Make it yours

Open **Extras → Settings** to change the player. A few places to start:

- **Home Screen:** choose which items appear on Home and in Extras.
- **Playback → EQ:** adjust the sound or choose a preset.
- **Now Playing:** change the idle display, cassette theme or lyrics size.
- **Appearance:** choose an accent colour and customize Control Center, which also has Wi-Fi Transfer and Screenshot tiles.

To return to Shanling Home, open **Settings → Developer → Startup → Open Shanling Home**. Tap the
M1+ Music icon on Shanling Home to return.
